Construction safety notes

Personal study notes in a side panel, built from public regulations, OSHA investigation reports and enforcement records. Unofficial: not from OSHA or any course provider, and not legal advice.

  1. Show the Favorites Bar if it's hidden (Shift-Command-B).
  2. Drag this button onto the Favorites Bar: Safety Notes
  3. On the page you're reading, click Safety Notes. Click it again to close.

The panel only adds itself to the page. It sends no input and doesn't read or change anything else there.
